Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

What the tool accesses

With the owner's permission, the tool uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API with two scopes: user.info.basic (display name, to confirm which account is connected) and video.upload (to upload video drafts). It does not access data of any other TikTok user.

Where data is stored

Access tokens are stored only locally on the owner's computer and are never shared.

Other services

To rate clips, the transcript of a clip and public Twitch chat messages are sent to the Anthropic Claude API. Clips are created with the owner's Twitch account.

What the tool does not do

No tracking, no advertising, no sale or sharing of personal data.

Revoking access

The owner can remove the tool's access at any time in the TikTok app settings and delete the local token file.
