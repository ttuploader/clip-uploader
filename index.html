Clip Uploader is a private, non-commercial desktop tool. It turns Twitch clips into vertical videos with subtitles and uploads them as drafts to its owner's own TikTok account through the official TikTok Content Posting API.

Nothing is published automatically: the owner reviews every draft in the TikTok app and decides whether to post it. The tool is not offered to anyone else.
